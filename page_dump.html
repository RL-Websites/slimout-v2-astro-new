Important compliance requirements

Day 0 lab

Must be completed before any prescription review or medication shipment.

Day 90 lab

A reminder is sent 15 days before. This lab must be completed before Day 90, allowing the doctor 2–3 days to review results. Month 4 medication will not ship without this lab.

Day 120 lab

Another mandatory follow-up lab to monitor hormone safety and stability.

Important

Without these lab results, our doctors cannot proceed with your prescription.
